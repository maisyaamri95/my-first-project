# Pelajaran 3: Nombor, Hari & Bulan

🎯 **Matlamat:** Boleh sebut nombor, hari, bulan dan masa.

## 1. Nombor 0–20

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 zero | 1 one | 2 two | 3 three |
| 4 four | 5 five | 6 six | 7 seven |
| 8 eight | 9 nine | 10 ten | 11 eleven |
| 12 twelve | 13 thirteen | 14 fourteen | 15 fifteen |
| 16 sixteen | 17 seventeen | 18 eighteen | 19 nineteen |
| 20 twenty | | | |

> 💡 Nombor **13–19** semua berakhir dengan **-teen**.

## 2. Puluhan

| 10 ten | 20 twenty | 30 thirty | 40 **forty** (bukan "fourty"!) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 fifty | 60 sixty | 70 seventy | 80 eighty |
| 90 ninety | 100 one hundred | 1,000 one thousand | |

**Cara gabung:** puluhan + sengkang + sa
- 21 = twenty-one
- 35 = thirty-five
- 99 = ninety-nine

> ⚠️ **Hati-hati:** **thirteen (13)** vs **thirty (30)**. Untuk *-teen*, tekan bunyi di hujung: "thir-**TIIN**". Untuk *-ty*, tekan di depan: "**THIR**-ti".

## 3. Hari Dalam Seminggu (Days)

| Bahasa Inggeris | Maksud |
|-----------------|--------|
| Monday | Isnin |
| Tuesday | Selasa |
| Wednesday | Rabu (sebut: "wenz-dei") |
| Thursday | Khamis |
| Friday | Jumaat |
| Saturday | Sabtu |
| Sunday | Ahad |

> 💡 Nama hari dan bulan **sentiasa huruf besar** di depan: **M**onday, **J**anuary.

## 4. Bulan (Months)

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| January – Januari | February – Februari | March – Mac |
| April – April | May – Mei | June – Jun |
| July – Julai | August – Ogos | September – September |
| October – Oktober | November – November | December – Disember |

## 5. Perkataan Masa

- **today** = hari ini
- **tomorrow** = esok
- **yesterday** = semalam
- **week** = minggu, **month** = bulan, **year** = tahun

## 6. Bertanya Masa ⏰

**A:** What time is it? *(Pukul berapa sekarang?)*
**B:** It's three o'clock. *(Pukul tiga.)*

| Masa | Cara sebut |
|------|-----------|
| 3:00 | three o'clock |
| 3:15 | three fifteen / quarter past three |
| 3:30 | three thirty / half past three |
| 3:45 | three forty-five / quarter to four |
| pagi | a.m. — contoh: 8 a.m. |
| petang/malam | p.m. — contoh: 8 p.m. |

## ✏️ Latihan

5. "Esok" dalam Bahasa Inggeris ialah ________.
