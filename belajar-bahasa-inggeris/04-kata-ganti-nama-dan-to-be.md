# Pelajaran 4: Kata Ganti Nama & "To Be" (am / is / are)

🎯 **Matlamat:** Faham perkataan **paling penting** dalam Bahasa Inggeris: *am, is, are*.

## 1. Kata Ganti Nama (Pronouns)

| Bahasa Inggeris | Maksud |
|-----------------|--------|
| **I** | Saya (sentiasa huruf besar!) |
| **You** | Awak / Kamu / Anda |
| **He** | Dia (lelaki) |
| **She** | Dia (perempuan) |
| **It** | Ia (benda / binatang) |
| **We** | Kami / Kita |
| **They** | Mereka |

> 💡 Dalam Bahasa Melayu, "dia" untuk lelaki dan perempuan. Dalam Bahasa Inggeris, **lelaki = he**, **perempuan = she**.

## 2. "To Be" — am / is / are

Dalam Bahasa Melayu kita kata *"Saya guru"*. Dalam Bahasa Inggeris, **wajib** ada perkataan penghubung: *"I **am** a teacher."*

### 🔑 Formula (hafal ini!)

| Pronoun | To Be | Singkatan | Contoh |
|---------|-------|-----------|--------|
| I | **am** | I'm | I am happy. *(Saya gembira.)* |
| He / She / It | **is** | he's / she's / it's | She is a doctor. *(Dia doktor.)* |
| You / We / They | **are** | you're / we're / they're | They are students. *(Mereka pelajar.)* |

> 🧠 **Cara ingat:** **I** → **am**. Seorang (he/she/it) → **is**. Ramai atau "you" → **are**.

## 3. Bila Guna "To Be"?

1. **Nama / pekerjaan:** I am Ahmad. He is a driver.
2. **Perasaan / keadaan:** We are tired. She is sick.
3. **Umur:** I am 30 years old.
4. **Tempat:** They are at home. *(Mereka di rumah.)*
5. **Sifat:** The car is red. *(Kereta itu merah.)*

## 4. Ayat Negatif — tambah **not**

| Positif | Negatif |
|---------|---------|
| I am hungry. | I am **not** hungry. (I'm not) |
| He is busy. | He is **not** busy. (He **isn't**) |
| They are here. | They are **not** here. (They **aren't**) |

## 5. Ayat Soalan — terbalikkan susunan

| Penyata | Soalan |
|---------|--------|
| You **are** a student. | **Are** you a student? |
| She **is** at school. | **Is** she at school? |

**Jawapan pendek:**
- Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

## 6. a / an — "seorang / sebuah"

- **a** sebelum bunyi konsonan: **a** book, **a** car, **a** teacher
- **an** sebelum bunyi vokal (a, e, i, o, u): **an** apple, **an** egg, **an** umbrella

## ✏️ Latihan

Isi dengan **am / is / are**:

1. I ____ a student.
2. She ____ my sister.
3. We ____ friends.
4. The cat ____ black.
5. They ____ not at home.

Tukar kepada soalan:

6. He is a doctor. → ____________?

Pilih **a** atau **an**:

7. ____ orange   8. ____ house
