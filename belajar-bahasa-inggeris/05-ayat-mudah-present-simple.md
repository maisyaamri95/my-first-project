# Pelajaran 5: Ayat Mudah — Present Simple

🎯 **Matlamat:** Boleh bercakap tentang **rutin harian** dan **perkara yang biasa berlaku**.

## 1. Susunan Ayat Bahasa Inggeris

Sama macam Bahasa Melayu! 🎉

```
Siapa   +   Buat apa   +   Apa / Di mana
(Subjek)    (Kata kerja)   (Objek)

  I     +     eat      +   rice.
 Saya         makan         nasi.
```

## 2. Kata Kerja Asas (Verbs)

| Verb | Maksud | Verb | Maksud |
|------|--------|------|--------|
| eat | makan | drink | minum |
| sleep | tidur | wake up | bangun |
| go | pergi | come | datang |
| work | bekerja | study | belajar |
| read | baca | write | tulis |
| like | suka | want | mahu |
| have | ada / mempunyai | speak | bercakap |
| watch | tonton | play | main |

## 3. ⭐ Peraturan Paling Penting: Tambah **-s**

Bila subjek ialah **he / she / it** (seorang/satu benda), kata kerja **tambah -s**.

| Subjek | Contoh |
|--------|--------|
| I / You / We / They | I **eat** rice. They **play** football. |
| He / She / It | He **eats** rice. She **plays** football. |

### Ejaan khas:
- Berakhir **-o, -sh, -ch, -ss, -x** → tambah **-es**: go → go**es**, watch → watch**es**
- Berakhir konsonan + **y** → tukar ke **-ies**: study → stud**ies**
- **have** → **has** (khas!): She **has** a cat.

## 4. Ayat Negatif — guna **don't / doesn't**

| Subjek | Negatif | Contoh |
|--------|---------|--------|
| I / You / We / They | **don't** + verb | I **don't** like durian. |
| He / She / It | **doesn't** + verb (tiada -s) | She **doesn't** like durian. |

> ⚠️ Selepas **doesn't**, buang -s: ~~She doesn't likes~~ ❌ → She doesn't **like** ✅

## 5. Ayat Soalan — guna **Do / Does**

| Soalan | Jawapan pendek |
|--------|----------------|
| **Do** you like coffee? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| **Does** he work here? | Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. |

## 6. Perkataan Kekerapan

| | Maksud | |
|---|---|---|
| **always** | selalu (100%) | I always drink water. |
| **usually** | biasanya | I usually wake up at 6. |
| **sometimes** | kadang-kadang | I sometimes cook. |
| **never** | tidak pernah (0%) | I never smoke. |
| **every day** | setiap hari | I study English every day. |

## 7. Contoh: Rutin Harian Saya

> I wake up at 6 a.m. every day. I take a shower and I eat breakfast. I usually drink tea. I go to work at 8. My sister doesn't work. She studies at university. In the evening, I watch TV. I sleep at 11 p.m.

## ✏️ Latihan

Betulkan kata kerja dalam kurungan:

1. She ______ (eat) bread every morning.
2. We ______ (go) to school by bus.
3. My father ______ (watch) the news.
4. He ______ (not / like) fish.
5. ______ you speak English?
6. ______ she have a car?

7. Tulis 3 ayat tentang rutin harian **anda**.
