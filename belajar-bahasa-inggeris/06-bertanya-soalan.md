# Pelajaran 6: Bertanya Soalan (WH-Questions)

🎯 **Matlamat:** Boleh bertanya soalan untuk dapatkan maklumat.

## 1. Perkataan Soalan

| Bahasa Inggeris | Maksud | Contoh |
|-----------------|--------|--------|
| **What** | Apa | What is your name? *(Siapa nama awak?)* |
| **Where** | Di mana | Where do you live? *(Awak tinggal di mana?)* |
| **When** | Bila | When is your birthday? *(Bila hari jadi awak?)* |
| **Who** | Siapa | Who is she? *(Siapa dia?)* |
| **Why** | Kenapa | Why are you sad? *(Kenapa awak sedih?)* |
| **How** | Bagaimana | How are you? *(Apa khabar?)* |
| **Which** | Yang mana | Which one do you want? *(Awak nak yang mana?)* |

> 💡 Perhatikan: *"What is your name?"* diterjemah sebagai **"Siapa** nama awak?", bukan "Apa nama awak". Bahasa tak selalu sama perkataan demi perkataan!

## 2. "How" + Perkataan Lain

| Soalan | Maksud | Contoh jawapan |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **How much** is this? | Berapa harga ini? | It's 10 ringgit. |
| **How many** children do you have? | Berapa ramai anak awak? | I have two. |
| **How old** are you? | Berapa umur awak? | I'm 25. |
| **How long** is the trip? | Berapa lama perjalanan? | Two hours. |

> 💡 **How much** = untuk harga / benda tak boleh dikira (air, duit).
> **How many** = untuk benda boleh dikira (orang, buku, kereta).

## 3. Formula Bina Soalan

### Dengan **to be** (am/is/are):
```
WH-word + am/is/are + subjek ?
 Where  +    is     + the toilet?
```

### Dengan kata kerja lain:
```
WH-word + do/does + subjek + verb ?
 What   +   do    +  you   + want?
 Where  +  does   +  he    + work?
```

## 4. Soalan Berguna Setiap Hari 🌟

- **Where is the toilet?** — Tandas di mana?
- **How much is this?** — Berapa harga ini?
- **What does this mean?** — Apa maksud ini?
- **Can you help me?** — Boleh tolong saya?
- **Can you speak slowly, please?** — Boleh cakap perlahan sikit?
- **Can you repeat that, please?** — Boleh ulang sekali lagi?
- **How do you say "kucing" in English?** — Apa Bahasa Inggeris untuk "kucing"?

> ⭐ Tiga soalan terakhir sangat penting untuk pelajar! Jangan malu guna.

## ✏️ Latihan

Pilih perkataan soalan yang betul:

1. ______ is your birthday? — In May.
2. ______ do you live? — In Ipoh.
3. ______ is that man? — He is my uncle.
4. ______ are you late? — Because of the traffic.
5. ______ much is the shirt? — 30 ringgit.
6. ______ many brothers do you have? — Three.

Susun perkataan menjadi soalan:

7. does / work / where / she → ______________?
