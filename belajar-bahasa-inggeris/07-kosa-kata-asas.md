# Pelajaran 7: Kosa Kata Asas (Basic Vocabulary)

🎯 **Matlamat:** Tahu perkataan yang paling kerap digunakan setiap hari.

> 💡 **Tips hafal:** Belajar **5 perkataan sehari**. Tulis di kertas kecil dan tampal di tempat yang selalu nampak (peti sejuk, cermin).

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Keluarga (Family)

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| father / dad – ayah | mother / mum – ibu | brother – abang/adik lelaki | sister – kakak/adik perempuan |
| son – anak lelaki | daughter – anak perempuan | husband – suami | wife – isteri |
| grandfather – datuk | grandmother – nenek | uncle – pak cik | aunt – mak cik |
| cousin – sepupu | baby – bayi | parents – ibu bapa | children – anak-anak |

> 💡 *Older brother* = abang, *younger brother* = adik lelaki.

## 🎨 Warna (Colours)

red – merah · blue – biru · green – hijau · yellow – kuning · black – hitam · white – putih · orange – jingga/oren · pink – merah jambu · purple – ungu · brown – coklat · grey – kelabu

> ⚠️ **Susunan berbeza!** Bahasa Melayu: kereta **merah**. Bahasa Inggeris: a **red** car. Sifat (adjektif) **di depan** benda.

## 🍚 Makanan & Minuman (Food & Drinks)

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| rice – nasi | bread – roti | egg – telur |
| chicken – ayam | fish – ikan | beef – daging lembu |
| vegetables – sayur | fruit – buah | noodles – mi |
| water – air | tea – teh | coffee – kopi |
| milk – susu | sugar – gula | salt – garam |

## 🏠 Tempat (Places)

house / home – rumah · school – sekolah · office – pejabat · hospital – hospital · shop – kedai · market – pasar · bank – bank · mosque – masjid · restaurant – restoran · park – taman · airport – lapangan terbang · bus stop – perhentian bas

## 😊 Perasaan (Feelings)

happy – gembira · sad – sedih · angry – marah · tired – penat · hungry – lapar · thirsty – dahaga · scared – takut · bored – bosan · sick – sakit · excited – teruja

Contoh: *I am **hungry**. Let's eat!* (Saya lapar. Jom makan!)

## ↔️ Perkataan Berlawanan (Opposites)

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| big ↔ small (besar ↔ kecil) | hot ↔ cold (panas ↔ sejuk) | fast ↔ slow (laju ↔ perlahan) |
| new ↔ old (baru ↔ lama) | good ↔ bad (baik ↔ buruk) | easy ↔ difficult (senang ↔ susah) |
| open ↔ close (buka ↔ tutup) | cheap ↔ expensive (murah ↔ mahal) | happy ↔ sad (gembira ↔ sedih) |
