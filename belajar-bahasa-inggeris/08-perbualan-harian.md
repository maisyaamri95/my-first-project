# Pelajaran 8: Perbualan Harian (Daily Conversations)

🎯 **Matlamat:** Guna semua yang dah dipelajari dalam situasi sebenar.

> 💡 **Cara berlatih:** Baca dialog dengan kuat. Lebih baik lagi — berlatih dengan kawan, seorang jadi A, seorang jadi B. Kemudian tukar peranan.

## 🛒 Situasi 1: Di Kedai

**Pekerja:** Hello! Can I help you? *(Boleh saya bantu?)*
**Anda:** Yes, please. I'm looking for a T-shirt. *(Ya. Saya cari baju-T.)*
**Pekerja:** What size? *(Saiz apa?)*
**Anda:** Medium, please.
**Pekerja:** Here you are. *(Ini dia.)*
**Anda:** How much is it? *(Berapa harganya?)*
**Pekerja:** It's 25 ringgit.
**Anda:** OK, I'll take it. *(Baik, saya ambil.)*
**Pekerja:** Thank you!

**Frasa berguna:**
- Can I try it on? — Boleh saya cuba pakai?
- Do you have a bigger size? — Ada saiz lebih besar?
- It's too expensive. — Mahal sangat.
- Can I pay by card? — Boleh bayar guna kad?

## 🍽️ Situasi 2: Di Restoran

**Pelayan:** Good evening! Table for how many? *(Meja untuk berapa orang?)*
**Anda:** For two, please.
**Pelayan:** What would you like to order? *(Nak pesan apa?)*
**Anda:** I would like fried rice and an iced tea, please. *(Saya nak nasi goreng dan teh ais.)*
**Pelayan:** Anything else? *(Ada lagi?)*
**Anda:** No, thank you.
...
**Anda:** Excuse me, can I have the bill, please? *(Maaf, boleh minta bil?)*

**Frasa berguna:**
- I would like... / I'd like... — Saya mahu... (cara sopan)
- Not spicy, please. — Tak nak pedas.
- It's delicious! — Sedap!

## 🗺️ Situasi 3: Tanya Jalan

**Anda:** Excuse me. Where is the train station? *(Tumpang tanya. Stesen kereta api di mana?)*
**Orang:** Go straight and turn left at the traffic lights. *(Jalan terus dan belok kiri di lampu isyarat.)*
**Anda:** Is it far? *(Jauh ke?)*
**Orang:** No, it's about five minutes' walk. *(Tak, lebih kurang lima minit berjalan kaki.)*
**Anda:** Thank you very much!
**Orang:** You're welcome.

**Arah:**
| | |
|---|---|
| go straight – jalan terus | turn left – belok kiri |
| turn right – belok kanan | next to – sebelah |
| opposite – bertentangan | behind – belakang |
| in front of – depan | near – dekat |

## 📞 Situasi 4: Di Telefon

**A:** Hello?
**B:** Hello, can I speak to Encik Rahman, please? *(Boleh saya bercakap dengan Encik Rahman?)*
**A:** Sorry, he's not here right now. Can I take a message? *(Maaf, dia tiada sekarang. Nak tinggal pesanan?)*
**B:** Yes, please tell him Aminah called. *(Ya, tolong beritahu Aminah telefon.)*
**A:** Sure. *(Baik.)*

## 🆘 Bila Anda Tak Faham

Ini **penyelamat** anda! Jangan pura-pura faham — tanya saja:

- **Sorry, I don't understand.** — Maaf, saya tak faham.
- **Can you speak slowly, please?** — Boleh cakap perlahan sikit?
- **Can you say that again?** — Boleh ulang?
- **My English is not very good.** — Bahasa Inggeris saya tak berapa bagus.

## ✏️ Latihan

Apa anda akan cakap dalam situasi ini?

1. Anda nak tahu harga sebuah beg.
2. Anda nak minta bil di restoran.
3. Anda tak faham apa orang itu cakap.
4. Anda nak pesan kopi dengan sopan.
5. Orang beri arah: *"Turn right."* Apa maksudnya?
