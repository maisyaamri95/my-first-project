# 🏆 Ujian Akhir (Final Test)

Tahniah kerana sampai ke sini! Cuba jawab **20 soalan** ini tanpa melihat nota. Kira markah anda di hujung.

## Bahagian A: Pilih jawapan yang betul (1 markah setiap satu)

1. She ____ a nurse.  (a) am (b) is (c) are
2. They ____ happy.  (a) am (b) is (c) are
3. I have ____ apple.  (a) a (b) an
4. He ____ to work by car.  (a) go (b) goes (c) going
5. ____ you like chocolate?  (a) Do (b) Does (c) Are
6. My brother ____ like vegetables.  (a) don't (b) doesn't (c) isn't
7. ____ is the toilet?  (a) What (b) Who (c) Where
8. ____ much is this book?  (a) How (b) What (c) Which
9. "Good night" digunakan bila...  (a) berjumpa (b) berpisah
10. 40 dalam perkataan:  (a) fourty (b) forty

## 🚀 Apa Seterusnya?

Bila anda sudah yakin dengan modul ini, belajar topik seterusnya:
- **Past Simple** — bercakap tentang semalam (*I went, I ate*)
- **Future** — bercakap tentang esok (*I will go, I am going to eat*)
- **Present Continuous** — apa yang sedang berlaku (*I am eating now*)
